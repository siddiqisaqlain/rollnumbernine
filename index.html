    [ How I work ]

    From site to screen.

    1. 01Observe

      Walk the site and sketch it by hand before anything else.

    2. 02Abstract

      Strip the form down to what actually matters.

    3. 03Design

      Develop it on progress sheets, from bubble diagrams to plans.

    4. 04Document

      Photograph, map and film the work and the place.

    5. 05Edit

      Cut it into posts and films people actually watch.

    [ 04 — About ]

    Roll number nine.

    I’m Shahid Nazir Khan, an architecture student from Kashmir. I think on paper first, with hand sketches, abstractions and progress sheets, and then I take the work out to the street. I photograph, film and edit what I find, from the timber khanqahs of Pampore to the lattice craft of pinjrakari.

    Architecture teaches me how a space is put together. Editing teaches me how to show it to someone else. This portfolio is where the two meet.
